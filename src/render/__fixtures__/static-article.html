The Rise of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence has transformed the technology landscape over the past decade. From natural language processing to computer vision, AI systems now perform tasks that were once thought to require human intelligence.

Machine learning, a subset of AI, enables systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. Deep learning, in turn, uses neural networks with many layers to extract features from raw data automatically.

The applications are vast: self-driving cars, medical diagnosis, financial fraud detection, personalised recommendations, and language translation. Each year, new breakthroughs push the boundaries of what is possible.

However, AI also raises important ethical questions around bias, privacy, job displacement, and accountability. Researchers and policymakers are working to develop frameworks that ensure AI systems are fair, transparent, and beneficial to all of society.

Looking ahead, the integration of AI into everyday tools will accelerate. The challenge is not just technical but social: how do we harness this power responsibly while ensuring that the benefits are shared equitably across communities and nations?
